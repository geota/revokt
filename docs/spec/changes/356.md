- **#356:** the public `sync()`, `syncAsync()` and `syncBlocking()` in revokt-jsse.
  - §4: `SyncResult`, `SyncOutcome`, `SyncFailure`, `RevoktWarning` and `WarningCode` are public, `sync()` their
    first consumer; their builders stay internal.
  - §4 `sync`: a sync that throws ends `FAILED` with `INTERNAL_ERROR` and the throwable as its cause; one that never
    ran (its clock read threw, or the start task's store step threw a fatal error) completes the call with that
    throwable. `syncBlocking()` on the loader thread throws `IllegalStateException` instead of waiting for itself, and
    an interrupt throws `InterruptedException` and leaves the sync running.
  - §5.1 "The sync schedule": a sync while the start task's store read stays unresolved ends `FAILED` with
    `STORE_FAILED`.
