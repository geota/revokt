- **#356:** the public `sync()`, `syncAsync()` and `syncBlocking()` in revokt-jsse.
  - §4: `SyncResult`, `SyncOutcome`, `SyncFailure`, `RevoktWarning` and `WarningCode` are public, `sync()` their
    first consumer; their builders stay internal.
  - §4 `sync`: a sync that throws before it ends is `FAILED` with `INTERNAL_ERROR` and the throwable as its cause; a
    throw after it ended keeps its result, and the next sync is in 15 min. A call whose sync never ran (its clock read
    threw, or the start task's store step threw a fatal error) completes with that throwable, a clock's
    `InterruptedException` wrapped in `IllegalStateException`. A completion the I/O executor throws on, or would run
    inline on the loader, runs on the common pool. `syncBlocking()` on the loader thread throws
    `IllegalStateException` instead of waiting for itself, and an interrupt throws `InterruptedException` and leaves
    the sync running. `close()` fails a pending call of every form with `IllegalStateException("closed")`, the suspend
    `sync()` included.
  - §5.1 "The sync schedule": a sync while the start task's store read stays unresolved ends `FAILED` with
    `STORE_FAILED`. An explicit `sync()` during the re-read's backoff re-reads at once, and the next wait doubles
    from it.
