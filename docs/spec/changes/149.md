- **#149, the weekly seed job:**
  - §7.2 data artifacts: a released seed is never older than the last released seed of its channel. The weekly job
    refuses a signed `<ts>` older than that release's attested `<ts>`, and releases nothing when every channel's `<ts>`
    equals it, as Firefox's Remote Settings client ignores a changeset older than its verified local data.
  - §6.5 the writer: the weekly job pins the Temurin 21 build whose zlib deflates the archive, and records it in its
    attestation.
