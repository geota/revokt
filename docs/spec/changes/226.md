- **#226 (audit B-F7, A-9), one work budget, the first sound path, no unrelated loop:**
  - §5.1 limits: the walk has one work budget, as webpki's `Budget` and mozilla::pkix's `buildForwardCallBudget`:
    `MAX_EXPLORED`, now 256, counts every candidate explored at any depth, anchors, memo hits and candidates the
    issuer rules refuse included. Each exploration makes at most one verifier call, so the old 256-verification bound
    stands. The 250,000 name-constraint comparisons are a second unit in the same budget. `MAX_VERIFICATIONS` and
    `MAX_RELATED_PER_LEVEL` are removed.
  - §4: `PathReason.EXPLORATION_BUDGET` is the one budget reason, for a spent walk and the step-3 presented cut.
    `RELATED_BUDGET`, `VERIFICATION_BUDGET` and the public `WalkEnd` are removed, and with it `RevoktStatus.walkEnds`.
    `endReason` and `endDepth` leave the `DEBUG` line, and `walkStats` keeps `verifications` and `candidatesExplored`.
  - §5.1 termination rule: the first sound path ends the walk at every depth, the leaf included, as both references
    stop. A walker `Found` carries one path and one leaf-issuer key. A `Budget` carries no path, so on a `Budget` only a
    fallback path's key is evaluated. `pathsFound` is 0 or 1 in M1.
  - §5.1 deferral rule: only related certificates are candidates, at every depth. The unrelated loop and
    `CandidateIndex.customCanonicaliser` are removed; a custom delegate's misclassified issuer is left to the PKIX
    fallback.
  - §5.1: a candidate whose raw subject and raw SPKI match a certificate on the path, the leaf included, is a loop. It
    is skipped like a certificate on the path, before any work is charged (RFC 4158 §5.2, as webpki and mozilla::pkix
    compare).
  - §10: the issuer-identity bullet, residual 1 (custom delegates), residual 4 (the budget) and residual 9 (CPU) follow.
    Residual 15 widens: an unconstrained CA's certificate for a recovered key decides the one key whenever its path
    comes first in candidate order, the honest intermediate presented after it included, as in mozilla::pkix (webpki
    goes on past a revoked path). The same order picks between two SPKI encodings of one honest issuer key, and M2's
    presented-before-preload order does not close it.
  - Test plan: rows (e), (i), (k), (l), (m), (o), (s1), (s2), (t), (y), (y2), (z), (ac), (ad), (ag), (ah), (aj), (an),
    (ap), (aq) and (as) follow the new rules, with loop cases added to (as); the reference walker offers related
    candidates only and also reports its keys over name-related links. P2 becomes a bracket, P3 leaves
    `Recover(leaf, CertifiedBy)` to residual 15, P4 lets the order pick the key under it, and P5 reads the one budget.
