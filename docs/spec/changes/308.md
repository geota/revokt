- **#308:** §7.2 data artifacts: before it compares the new seed with the last released one, the weekly job verifies
  that release's content signature again and requires the verified `<ts>` to equal the attested one. It checks only
  the parts that do not depend on the seed format, as Firefox re-verifies its local data: `seed.json`'s channel, the
  x5u chain to Mozilla's pins, the raw signature over `records.json`, the canonical payload, and the x5u validity at
  the signed `<ts>`. It checks no schema: the content signature names no collection, and the attestation binds the
  jar to the job that fetched `cert-revocations` and schema-checked it in its full self-load. A previous release that
  does not verify fails the job, where Firefox's client imports the older changeset.
