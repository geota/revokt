- **#144, the walker fuzzer's properties as the code after #226 meets them:**
  - Test plan §9.1 P2: `Budget ⇒` no leaf key was true by construction; it is now stated on the decision: a
    `PATH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` decision's keys are empty or the fallback path's one key, which is in `refKeys`.
  - P3 defines "honest" (the world with its world mutations only) and "a limit was hit" (a `Budget` walk or a
    `PATH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` decision of either world, `FALLBACK_LIMIT` included, which attacker certificates reach by
    taking a chain without a path past `FALLBACK_MAX_PRESENTED`). It also leaves out `Recover(child, CertifiedBy)` of
    any child when the honest walk is `NotFound`: the anchor-certified CA can supply the path that `DropIntermediate`
    or `UnsupportedOwnSig` removed, the walk finds the honest key, and the verdict follows that key's CRLite answer in
    either direction.
  - P8: `UnsupportedOwnSig` and `DropIntermediate` are `NotFound` walks, never `Budget`. `DropIntermediate`'s reason
    is the leaf level's: `NO_CANDIDATE` when the leaf's own issuer was dropped, `UNANCHORED` when a higher
    intermediate was, where the text said `NO_CANDIDATE` for both. The decision is `NO_ISSUER` for a chain of at most
    `FALLBACK_MAX_PRESENTED` certificates and `PATH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED(FALLBACK_LIMIT)` above it, where the text said
    "never `PATH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`".
  - §10 residual 16 (new): a chain of more than 8 presented certificates whose walk finds no path is
    `PATH_BUDGET_EXCEEDED(FALLBACK_LIMIT)`, which `DEFAULT` rejects. This is narrower than mozilla::pkix, which builds
    at most 6 intermediates (`MAX_SUBCA_COUNT`), so Firefox refuses every path longer than 8 certificates.
