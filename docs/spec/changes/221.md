- **#221 (audit B-F3, B-F10), the public surface is what a caller of `evaluate` touches:**
  - §4: the first-consumer rule. A type, member or enum value is public only once a public member produces or
    consumes it. Until then it is `internal`, or `@RevoktInternalApi` where another revokt module uses it.
  - §4: output types (`Verdict.*`, every `Finding`, `ExemptionContext`, `PresentedChain`, `PolicyDiagnostic`,
    `FetchRequest`) have internal builders and `newBuilder()`; their test factories come with `revokt-testing` in
    0.3. `FetchRequest`'s builder stays `@RevoktTestingApi` for fetcher tests. Input types (`RevoktPolicy`,
    `MozillaRemoteSettings`, `EvaluationRequest`, `FetchResponse`) keep public builders. `AnchorSnapshot` leaves the
    output-type list.
  - §4, §11: `SyncResult`, `SyncOutcome`, `SyncFailure`, `RevoktWarning` and `WarningCode` are internal in 0.2,
    since `sync()` is, and go public with it in 0.3. This supersedes the "ship public" part of DECISIONS #2
    (revision 49, 2026-11-05).
  - §4: `NoIssuerReason`, `ParseReason`, `KeyabilityClause` and `TerminalKind` are internal for good: they are the
    DEBUG line's vocabulary. The 0.3 `RevoktStatus.noIssuer` and `parseFailed` counters are keyed by `PathReason`,
    the one public reason type.
  - §4: the values no main code produces are removed: `ChainSource.PRESENTED`, and `Freshness.LAST_VERIFIED_POLL`,
    `SEQUENCE_TIME` and `ARTIFACT_DATE` (M2 brings them back with their datasets).
  - §4: `CredentialType`, `ExemptionContext.credentialType` and `RevoktEvent.credentialType` are M5 additions.
    Until then `ExemptionContext.chain` is never null.
  - §4: `RevoktLimits` keeps public only the four caps the builder raises. `MAX_DEPTH` and the other limits are
    internal, and `EvaluationRequest.build()`'s message states the 17-certificate path limit. `MiB` and the limits of
    features not built yet (caches, events, read-only polling, `maxTrustManagers`, anchor refresh) are not declared.
  - §4: `MozillaRemoteSettings` has no public getters; a mirror is `DEFAULT.newBuilder()` plus its overrides.
  - §3: `RevoktStorage` is `@RevoktInternalApi` in 0.2 (no `storage(…)` setter). In 0.3 it is a sealed type built by
    `inMemory()`, `JvmStorage.directory(…)` and `AndroidStorage`'s factories, with internal members, and never
    implemented by a consumer. `StoreLockedException` is internal. The store design is unchanged.
  - §3: `PathBuilderFallback` and `AnchorSnapshot` are `@RevoktInternalApi` with `PlatformTrust` (#127). §3.2: the
    seam's data types (`FilterFormat`, `GenerationFiles`, `CrliteQuery`, `CrliteSct`, `JvmGenerationFiles`) are
    `@RevoktInternalApi` with the seam. `RevoktLifecycle` is `@RevoktInternalApi` until `RevoktState`.
  - §4's `@file:JvmName` rule now holds for `RevoktPolicy.kt` (`RevoktPolicyDurations`), `Staleness.kt`
    (`StalenessDurations`) and revokt-jsse's `RevoktDsl.kt` (`RevoktDsl`).
  - The test plan's `parseFailed` row is zero-filled over the `PARSE_FAILED` `PathReason`s.
