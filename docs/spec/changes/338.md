- **#338, the start chain's follow-ups:**
  - §4 S `SEED_PAST_REJECT_THRESHOLD`: the seed is the seed generation the instance serves once the start task's store
    step ends, so a restart that loads a stored seed generation (as `live`, as `previous` or adopted) reports it as a
    first start does, from that generation's signed `<ts>`, and a step that ends on a synced generation reports none.
    §11's 0.2 row logs it when the loader installs or loads a seed generation after `start()` returned.
  - §4 S sole-seed bullet: a start task whose store step leaves no generation after `start()` returned (a `live` that
    passed Load's steps 1 and 2 at `start()` fails step 3 or 4 beside a seed that does not install, or a store read
    throws and stays unresolved) logs one `ERROR` naming `SEED_UNREADABLE` and `NO_DATA`, except while `close()`
    interrupts the loader; with `state` it is `SEED_UNREADABLE` on `RevoktState.warnings` beside the dataset's
    `generationId == null` and `pending == false`.
  - §5.1 "The sync schedule": a sync whose re-read leaves the start task's store read unresolved waits §7.1's computed
    failure backoff over the consecutive re-reads, 15 min doubled up to 6 h, reset once the read resolves, instead of a
    flat 15 min.
