- **#338, the start chain's follow-ups:**
  - §4 S `SEED_PAST_REJECT_THRESHOLD`: the seed is the seed generation the instance serves, so a restart that loads a
    stored seed generation reports it as a first start does, from that generation's signed `<ts>`. §11's 0.2 row logs it
    when the loader installs or loads a seed generation after `start()` returned.
  - §4 S sole-seed bullet: a `live` that passes Load's steps 1 and 2 at `start()` but fails step 3 or 4 on the loader,
    beside a seed that does not install, leaves no generation after `start()` returned; it is logged at `ERROR` naming
    `SEED_UNREADABLE` and `NO_DATA`, and with `state` it is `SEED_UNREADABLE` on `RevoktState.warnings` beside the
    dataset's `generationId == null` and `pending == false`.
  - §5.1 "The sync schedule": a sync whose re-read leaves the start task's store read unresolved waits §7.1's computed
    failure backoff over the consecutive re-reads, 15 min doubled up to 6 h, reset once the read resolves, instead of a
    flat 15 min.
